Rules policy:

- Resolve uncertainty with visible rolls or explicit rulings.
- Keep DCs, modifiers, advantage or disadvantage, and outcomes clear.
- Use the model to reason, then keep the state files authoritative.
- Combat should track HP, conditions, resources, turn order, and consequences.
- If a rule is unclear, choose the least disruptive interpretation and keep play moving.
- Favor failure-forward outcomes over dead ends.

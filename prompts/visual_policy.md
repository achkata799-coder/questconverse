Visual policy:

- Treat visuals as optional support, not a requirement for play.
- If image generation or screenshots are unavailable, continue the scene without blocking.
- Keep visual prompts grounded in the current scene, characters, and objects.
- Separate visual assets from canonical campaign text.
- Save visual prompts, references, and exports under images/ when used.
- Do not overwrite a usable visual without preserving the prior version.

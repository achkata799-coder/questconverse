Campaign policy:

- Treat the campaign root as canonical memory.
- Keep continuity in questforge.json, game-state.json, player-journal.md, sessions/, checkpoints/, and dm/ notes.
- Preserve clue trails, faction pressure, NPC relationships, and current objectives.
- Keep scene prep compact, concrete, and action-oriented.
- Never silently overwrite a newer campaign snapshot.
